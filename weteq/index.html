WetEQ

A four-band British console equaliser, modelled as a circuit rather than as a frequency response, with the saturation, crosstalk and gain-dependent bandwidth of a large-format desk.

Download from wetvst.com Source code

Free and open source, MIT licence. VST3 for Windows, macOS and Linux, and an Audio Unit for Logic and GarageBand.

Take a closer look

Click a + to see what that control does.

Tap a +to see what that control does.

What it has

  • 4 bands (LF shelf, LMF bell, HMF bell, HF shelf)
  • HPF, LPF and input GAIN, 17 stepped positions per control - hold Shift for four times finer
  • Host sample rate, no downsampling
  • Real-time L/R input and output LED metering

Installation

Windows
Copy WetEQ.vst3 to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
macOS
Copy WetEQ.vst3 to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/, and WetEQ.component to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/ for Logic and GarageBand. Both are signed and notarised by Apple.
Linux
Copy WetEQ.vst3 to ~/.vst3/

Then restart your DAW and rescan for plug-ins.
