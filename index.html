Why WET VST?

Fully Open Source

Most free plugins never show their code. The free WET plugins do: the full source, under the MIT licence.

Pure VST3 SDK

Built directly on Steinberg's official VST3 SDK, with nothing in between, so the plugins stay small and start instantly.

Cross-Platform

Works in your DAW on Windows, macOS (Intel and Apple Silicon) and Linux, with an Audio Unit for Logic and GarageBand.

Premium

Premium plugins that go beyond the basics, with the same quality and ease of use.
