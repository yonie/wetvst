Privacy

Last updated 6 September 2026

The short version. The plugins collect nothing and connect to nothing. This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The only personal data processed anywhere is an email address, and only if you choose to give one.

Who is responsible

Ronald Klarenbeek, the Netherlands. Contact: contact@wetvst.com.

The plugins

WetDelay, WetReverb, WetEQ and WetCompressor run entirely on your own machine. There is no telemetry, no licence check, no update check and no network access of any kind. Nothing about how you use them reaches me or anyone else. The full source code is public under the MIT licence, so this is checkable rather than something you have to take on trust.

This website

No cookies. No analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising and no third-party scripts. Nothing is stored in your browser.

The site is served by a static hosting provider, which keeps ordinary server logs including visitors' IP addresses for security and abuse prevention. Those logs belong to the host and are kept under the host's own policy. I do not receive them and cannot identify individual visitors.

Release notices

If you sign up for release notices, I store your email address and nothing else. No name, no country, no what-you-downloaded.

  • What it is for: telling you when a WET plugin is released or updated. That is the whole purpose. No newsletters, no offers, no promotions and no mailings on behalf of anyone else.
  • Legal basis: your consent, under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR.
  • Confirmed opt-in: an address is only added to the list after you click the link in a confirmation email. Typing someone else's address into the form achieves nothing.
  • Who processes it: EmailOctopus, a mailing-list provider in the United Kingdom, acting only on my instructions under a data processing agreement. The United Kingdom is covered by an EU adequacy decision, so the transfer needs no separate safeguard.
  • No tracking: open tracking and click tracking are switched off. I do not know whether you read an email or which links you followed.
  • No profiling and no automated decision-making.
  • Never sold, rented or shared. Not with anyone, for any amount, ever.
  • Leaving: every email carries a one-click unsubscribe link. Your address is deleted when you unsubscribe. You can also just mail me and I will remove it.
  • How long: until you unsubscribe. There is no other expiry, because there is nothing else to expire.

Email you send me

If you write to contact@wetvst.com, your message and address sit in my mailbox so that I can reply, and stay there while the conversation is still useful. They are not added to any list and not used for anything else.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask me for a copy of the personal data I hold about you, or ask me to correct it, delete it, restrict it, hand it over in a portable form, or stop processing it. Mail contact@wetvst.com and I will deal with it. In practice, for almost everyone, the answer to "what do you hold about me" is either "nothing" or "one email address".

If you think I have handled your data badly, you can complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Children

This site is not aimed at children, and no data is knowingly collected from them.

Changes

If this statement changes, the date at the top changes with it. The page is version controlled in public, so the history is visible at github.com/yonie/wetvst.
