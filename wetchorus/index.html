WetChorus

Stereo chorus

A bucket-brigade stereo chorus, modelled as the circuit rather than as a delay line with an LFO on it, with the moving clock, compander breathing and audible noise floor of the chorus built into a 1970s solid-state guitar combo.

Download from wetvst.com Source code

Free and open source, MIT licence. VST3 for Windows, macOS and Linux, and an Audio Unit for Logic and GarageBand.

What it has

  • Its 2-position VIB/CHORUS switch, made continuous (19 positions)
  • Stepped SPEED, DEPTH and MODE, hold Shift for four times finer
  • Host sample rate, no downsampling
  • Real-time input and output LED metering

Installation

Windows
Copy WetChorus.vst3 to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
macOS
Copy WetChorus.vst3 to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/, and WetChorus.component to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/ for Logic and GarageBand. Both are signed and notarised by Apple.
Linux
Copy WetChorus.vst3 to ~/.vst3/

Then restart your DAW and rescan for plug-ins.
