WetDelay

Digital delay

A stereo delay with the character of an 80s rack-style digital unit: 24 kHz internal rate, 12-bit quantisation and a little crosstalk between the channels. It is 100% wet, made to sit on a send.

Download Source code

Free and open source, MIT licence. VST3 for Windows, macOS and Linux, and an Audio Unit for Logic and GarageBand.

What it has

  • 6 delay times (20ms to 400ms)
  • 24kHz internal rate, 12-bit quantization
  • Stereo with 1% channel crosstalk
  • Real-time input/output metering

Installation

Windows
Copy WetDelay.vst3 to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
macOS
Copy WetDelay.vst3 to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/, and WetDelay.component to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/ for Logic and GarageBand. Both are signed and notarised by Apple.
Linux
Copy WetDelay.vst3 to ~/.vst3/

Then restart your DAW and rescan for plug-ins.
