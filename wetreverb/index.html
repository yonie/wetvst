WetReverb

A stereo reverb with the sound of early digital racks: grainy, dense and full of character.

Download from wetvst.com Source code

Free and open source, MIT licence. VST3 for Windows, macOS and Linux, and an Audio Unit for Logic and GarageBand.

Take a closer look

What it has

  • 5 reverb modes (Room, Plate, Hall, Cathedral, Cosmos)
  • 24kHz internal rate, 12-bit quantization
  • Stereo with channel crosstalk
  • Real-time input/output LED metering

Installation

Windows
Copy WetReverb.vst3 to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
macOS
Copy WetReverb.vst3 to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/, and WetReverb.component to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/ for Logic and GarageBand. Both are signed and notarised by Apple.
Linux
Copy WetReverb.vst3 to ~/.vst3/

Then restart your DAW and rescan for plug-ins.
