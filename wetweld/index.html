WetWeld

Premium

Mastering processor

A mastering bus processor: gain, high-pass, colour, two shelving bands, high-frequency transient compression, stereo width and output gain in one fixed chain, modelled as a switched analogue bus box rather than assembled out of separate plug-ins.

Already bought it? Download from wetvst.com

EUR 15 including VAT. The licence code arrives by email and is entered once in the plug-in. No account, and the plug-in never goes online. Without a code it passes no audio.

What it has

  • 8 stages in 1 fixed chain (input GAIN, HPF, COLOR, LF shelf, HF shelf, COMPRESS, WIDEN, output GAIN)
  • 10 stepped controls, hold Shift for four times finer
  • Host sample rate, no downsampling
  • Real-time input, gain reduction and output LED metering

Installation

Windows
Copy WetWeld.vst3 to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
macOS
Copy WetWeld.vst3 to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/, and WetWeld.component to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/ for Logic and GarageBand. Both are signed and notarised by Apple.
Linux
Copy WetWeld.vst3 to ~/.vst3/

Then restart your DAW and rescan for plug-ins.
